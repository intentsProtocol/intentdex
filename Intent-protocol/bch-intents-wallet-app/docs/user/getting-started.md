# Getting started

BCH Intents Wallet is a self-custodial wallet for your phone. One recovery phrase gives you addresses on Bitcoin Cash, Ethereum and its networks, Solana and Bitcoin. Nobody else holds your keys, which also means nobody else can restore them: please read [Security and recovery](security-and-recovery.md) before you put real money in.

> **Alpha software.** The wallet has not been audited. Use testnet freely. On mainnet, use amounts you could afford to lose.

## Install

The alpha is installed from a file, not from a store. Open the build link you were given on your Android phone, download the APK, and allow the install when Android asks.

## Create a new wallet

1. **Create a new wallet.**
2. **Write down your recovery phrase.** Tap to reveal the 12 words and write them on paper, in order. This is the only backup of your wallet. The screen cannot be screenshotted, on purpose.
3. **Check your backup.** Type the three words the wallet asks for. If you would rather do this later, choose *Skip for now*: the wallet works, and a reminder stays on the Wallet tab until you back up.
4. **Choose a PIN.** Six digits, entered twice. The PIN opens the wallet on this phone.
5. **Turn on fingerprint or face unlock** if your phone has it, then **Open wallet**.

## Import a wallet you already have

1. **I already have a recovery phrase.**
2. Type or paste your 12 or 24 words. The wallet checks the phrase before going further.
3. Choose a PIN.

The same phrase gives the same addresses here as in the BCH Intents browser extension, MetaMask (Ethereum) and Phantom (Solana).

## A first look

| Where | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Account** (top left) | Your current account. Tap it to switch, rename or add accounts. |
| **Testnet / Mainnet** (top right) | Tap to switch. New wallets start on testnet. |
| **Scan** | Reads a QR code: an address to pay, or an app to connect. |
| **Lock** | Locks the wallet now. |
| **The card** | Your balance, and Send, Receive, Swap. |
| **Tokens** | Everything this account holds. Tap a token for details and actions. |
| **The bar at the bottom** | Wallet, Swap, Activity, Settings. |

Pull down on the Wallet tab to refresh.

## Try it on testnet

New wallets start on testnet, where coins are free and worth nothing. While the wallet is empty, the Wallet tab links to faucets:

- **BCH** for Bitcoin Cash chipnet
- **USDC** on the Ethereum test networks (Circle's faucet)
- **SOL** on Solana devnet
- **BTC** on Bitcoin testnet

Get some, then try [sending, receiving and swapping](using-the-wallet.md). When you are comfortable, switch to mainnet from the button at the top of the Wallet tab.

## Meet Cham

The chameleon is Cham. It looks around, shuts its eye while you type a PIN, and takes the colour of the chain you are working with.
