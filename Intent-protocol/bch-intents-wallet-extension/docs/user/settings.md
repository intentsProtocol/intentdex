# Settings

Open **Settings** from the bottom bar. Changes apply immediately and are remembered.

## Preferences

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | *Auto* follows your computer's light or dark mode. *Light* and *Dark* fix it. |
| **Language** | English or 简体中文. The Chinese text is a first draft and has not been reviewed by a native speaker; corrections are welcome. |
| **Open wallet as** | *Side panel* docks the wallet at the side of the browser when you click the toolbar icon. *Popup* opens the classic small window. |
| **Hide balances** | Masks every balance, for when someone can see your screen. The eye on the home screen does the same. |
| **Trading defaults** | See below. |

### Trading defaults

| Setting | Applies to | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Max slippage** (0.5%, 1%, 2%, 5%) | Solana swaps | The most the price may move against you between the quote and the trade. Beyond it, the trade is cancelled. Higher makes trades more likely to go through, at a possibly worse price. |
| **Auction length** (5 seconds to 5 minutes) | Bitcoin Cash swaps | How long solvers have to fill your order. Shorter returns unfilled funds sooner; longer gives more chances to fill. |

## Network

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mode** | *Testnet* or *Mainnet*, for every chain at once. Same as the pill at the top of the wallet. |
| **Networks** | Lists the networks the wallet uses in the current mode. |
| **Services** | Shows the swap services this build uses and whether each can be reached. Useful when a swap is unavailable. |

## Wallet

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Addresses** | Every address of the active account, with the derivation path other wallets use to reach it. Click one to copy it. |
| **Connected sites** | Sites you allowed to see your Bitcoin Cash address. Disconnect one or all. |
| **Hidden tokens** | Tokens you hid from the token list. Click *Show* to bring one back. |

## Privacy and security

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Auto-lock** | Locks the wallet after 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes without activity. |
| **Recovery phrase** | Shows your phrase after you enter your password. Says *Not backed up* until you confirm a backup. |
| **Change password** | Changes the password on this device. |

## Support

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Documentation** | Opens the documentation site. |
| **About** | The version you are running. |

## Danger zone

**Remove wallet** deletes the wallet from this browser. See [Security and recovery](security-and-recovery.md#removing-the-wallet).

## Lock wallet

The button at the bottom locks the wallet now.
